Strategy

Har side (BUY aur SELL) ki alag basket. BUY STOP market se $0.80 upar lagta hai aur girte gold ke saath neeche khiskta hai. Gold $0.80 palte to trade lagta hai.

Agla level pichhle trade se kam se kam $0.80 door. Lot 0.01 × 1.3 har level. Side ka profit $30 hote hi us side ke sab trade band.

App mode: START dabane par window (30/60/90 min) khulti hai. Window me hi nayi basket khulti hai, max 2 TP ke baad band. Window khatam hone par jo basket $1+ profit me ho wo turant band; loss wali basket chalti rehti hai aur $1+ profit aate hi band.

Shaant market check: pichhle 30 min me gold $5 se zyada hila ho to EA nayi basket nahi kholta.

Deep bounce: basket 8 level par pahunche to naye chhote level band; 0.20 lot ke max 2 bounce trade, side ka profit $15 par band, $4000 loss par band.

News: high-impact USD news se 30 min pehle aur 30 min baad nayi basket nahi.
